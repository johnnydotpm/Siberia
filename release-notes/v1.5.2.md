## What's new
- **Material 3 Expressive.** A third design next to Classic and Liquid Glass, in Settings → Appearance → Design. On Android 12 and later it can take its colours from your wallpaper. It brings Google Sans Flex and Material Symbols, a new navigation bar, a morphing loading indicator, a wavy progress bar on the lesson in progress, connected button groups, spring motion and the predictive back gesture on Android 14 and later. Home-screen widgets follow the wallpaper colours too.
- **Excuse absences in Librus.** From Today or Attendance, pick the days of the last two weeks or days to come, give a reason per day, and send the note straight to the class teacher after a confirmation. Siberia writes the note in Polish for you, or you write it in your own words. A history of sent notes shows whether each one is waiting, accepted or rejected, including notes from Librus's own e-Usprawiedliwienia module.
- **More for parents.** Sign-in asks who will use the app when a login has both a student's and a parent's account. The setup gets a Children step and parent tips. A parent's Today shows absences to excuse, recent notes, the next parents' meeting and when the day's lessons end. Choose the tabs of the bottom bar, and get an evening summary instead of separate notifications.

## Fixes
- Two lessons at the same time, such as a class split into groups, stand side by side instead of on top of each other, and a test of one group's lesson no longer shows on the other.
- The list under the events calendar starts after the day you pick instead of always from today.
- A grade of 0, which marks work not handed in, no longer pulls the average down.
- Excuse notes find the class teacher by the group Librus actually uses.
- The timetable day strip, navigation bar and lesson cards fit with very large text.
