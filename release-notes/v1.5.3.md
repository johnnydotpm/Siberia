## What's new
- **Lucky number.** Today can show the school's lucky number of the day from Librus, as a block you can turn on, off and move with the others. Schools without the module simply don't show it.
- **Picking messages, redone.** In every design, picking messages turns the top bar into the count with a way out and puts Read and Delete where the tabs were. On iOS 26 the Liquid Glass design uses UIKit's own toolbar and action sheet; Android draws the same.
- **Smoother everywhere.** Tabs fade through one another in Classic and Material 3 Expressive, as Material asks; Liquid Glass switches at once, as iOS does. Bars change smoothly when you pick messages, deleted messages fold away, and another folder, semester, period or month fades in. With reduced motion turned on in the system, everything changes at once.
- **One calendar.** Events, Attendance and the timetable's date picker share the same month calendar, with dots on days that have events or lessons.
- **Writing a message** sits beside the title in Material 3 Expressive and in the top bar in Classic, so the list starts higher. Expressive text fields are Material 3's filled ones.
- The update notice opens the latest release on GitHub when you tap it.

## Fixes
- Librus requests time out instead of leaving a sync spinning on a hung connection.
- A timetable week that arrives after you switch profiles no longer slips into the other profile.
- A message you open while a sync runs stays read after it.
- Signing out or removing a profile forgets its excuse notes.
- Resetting the settings also resets the wallpaper colours.
- Tapping a free day in the timetable strip opens that day, not the next one with lessons.
- Black theme: the page stays black, while cards, search fields and button groups get near-black tones and no longer disappear.
- Liquid Glass: no more empty gap at the top of the attendance calendar.
- Hidden tabs no longer keep animating, so the lesson progress wave doesn't drain the battery in the background.
- With very large text on narrow phones, profile marks, due dates, the All together summary and the next lesson's time no longer run past the edge.
- The timetable strip, the top bar on scroll, an empty day in Events, the fields of a new message and the Expressive Today tiles line up properly.
