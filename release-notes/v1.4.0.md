## What's new
- **Grade calculator.** Open a subject in Grades and tap the calculator: try out grades and see the new average, or pick a target and see how many grades of each value it takes to reach it. Weights are offered when your average is weighted.
- **Test reminders.** Settings → Notifications can remind you of tests the evening before (19:00 by default), the morning of, or both, with homework due that day if you want it. One notification per day lists everything due, in lesson order.

## Fixes
- Today on holidays shows the next lessons instead of "No upcoming lessons".
- The unread count stays on the Messages tab while it is selected.
- Long lesson and test descriptions scroll instead of being cut off; the Today blocks list scrolls in landscape.
- Sending a message or opening an attachment shows a readable error, inside the open sheet.
- The attendance calendar shows a day with an absence and a late arrival as partly absent, and switches months faster.
- The notification settings warn when the system doesn't allow notifications.
- Log out is unavailable while a sync runs, so an interrupted sync can't bring back the old account's data.
- No empty strip below the sign-in button.
