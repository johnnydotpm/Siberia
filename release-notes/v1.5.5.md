## What's new
- **Liquid Glass, closer to iOS 26.** On iPhone the switches, segmented controls, time picker, pop-up choices, folder menu and text edit menu are UIKit's own. On Android they are drawn to iOS 26's measure, with lenses of thick glass that bend and glint under your finger. Short notices float on glass over the tab bar, dialogs and the side menu are glass too, and the blur under the bars fades out without lines.
- **Messages, redone after Mail.** The folder is the page's title with a menu to switch folders. Rows show the sender's picture, the date and an unread dot, and long titles fit instead of being cut off.
- **Timetable cards, redone.** Room and teacher sit at one height on every card, a test shows at the end of its lesson, and the lesson on now shows the minutes left.
- **Librus Legacy plugin.** An optional plugin you can download for numeric Synergia logins: Add another diary → Librus Legacy. The usual Librus login stays the default.

## Fixes
- The side menu fits on an iPhone without scrolling.
- Cancel and Save in the excuse note's dialogs are in your language instead of showing internal keys.
