## What's new
- **More diaries through plugins.** Librus stays built in. Choose Fryderyk or MobiReg when adding another diary and Siberia downloads its plugin before you sign in. Each diary keeps its own account and data.
- **Several profiles, one app.** Add children, schools or accounts, then switch between them without signing out. Each profile keeps its own grades, homework and settings. Rename profiles and choose their colours to tell them apart.
- **All together.** View several profiles on one timetable and across the other tabs. Entries show which profile they belong to; messages are sent from the profile you choose.
- **Parent mode.** A parent-focused Today screen brings together each child's news and absences to excuse. Set quiet hours and an evening summary, or prepare an absence-excuse message to the class teacher, with a preview and confirmation before sending.
- **More control over messages.** A Sent folder shows recipients and available read receipts. Search by sender, subject or text, filter unread messages, and select messages to mark them read or delete them. Long-press a message for the same actions; deleting requires confirmation.
- **Attendance by subject and completed homework.** Check attendance for individual subjects, and mark homework done to keep the unfinished work in view.
- **First-run setup.** Choose accounts and notification preferences in the app, with a summary of your choices and a shortcut to widget setup. To try the app without a school account, hold the login-screen logo for five seconds to open an offline demo with fictional school data.
- **Widgets for your profiles.** Android widgets have separate profile settings, including All together. On iOS, choose the profile used by the Next lesson widget.

## Fixes
- The side menu and detail sheets stay still when their content fits, and scroll when it does not.
- iOS widget loading has been reworked for sideloaded installations, and the duplicate customizable Next lesson widget has been removed.
- Attendance entries can recover their subject from the timetable, including older cached lists.
- Polish letters in searches also match text typed without accents. Messages marked read stay in the unread-filter view until you leave it or change the filter.
- Dates and timetable weeks follow calendar days across midnight and daylight-saving changes; cached timestamps retain their correct time.
- Links in messages open with a tap, message drafts survive closing the composer, and errors stay inside the relevant sheet.
- Loading placeholders, timetable gestures, number updates and Liquid Glass scrolling are smoother. Notifications identify their profile, and background checks keep profiles separate.
